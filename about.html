The Collective / Origin File

Born Beyond the Fog.

In every trial, one side hunts and the other survives. We believe the competition, creativity and people behind those moments deserve a stage of their own.

Who We Are

A New Chapter in the Trials.

The Entity Collective is an independent, developing Dead by Daylight esports organization. Our ambition is to bring players, teams, streamers, coaches and fans together through organized competition and a community that values both skill and character. We are building the foundation now; official tournament operations and competitive programs will be introduced as they are ready.

Our Mission

Make competitive Dead by Daylight more approachable, better organized and more rewarding for the people who care about it.

Our Vision

Grow from accessible community events into a recognizable competitive ecosystem with seasonal competition, meaningful rankings and championship opportunities.

The Two Sides

Every Trial Has Two Stories.

The Survivors

Four players face the pressure together. Coordination, game sense and smart decisions can mean the difference between escape and sacrifice.

The Killer

One player controls the hunt. Map awareness, timing and tactical decisions create a different kind of competitive challenge.

TEC aims to respect both sides of the game while designing fair tournament formats with published rules and consistent scoring.
