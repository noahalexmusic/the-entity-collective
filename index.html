Who We Are

Born in the Fog. Built for Competition.

The Entity Collective is an emerging Dead by Daylight esports organization focused on building a competitive environment where players, teams, coaches, and content creators can grow.

Our mission is to create opportunities for competitors to demonstrate their skills through organized tournaments, structured competition, and community involvement.

We believe esports should be built on professionalism, competitive integrity, teamwork, and respect.
