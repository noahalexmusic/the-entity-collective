Who We Are

Born in the Fog. Built for Competition.

The Entity Collective is an emerging Dead by Daylight esports organization dedicated to building a competitive environment where players, teams, coaches, and creators can grow.

Our vision is to develop organized tournaments, seasonal rankings, competitive teams, and championships that give players meaningful opportunities to prove themselves.

We believe competitive gaming should bring people together through teamwork, professionalism, support, and growth.
