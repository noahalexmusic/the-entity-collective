Our Community

Dead by Daylight is better when players have people to learn with, compete alongside and celebrate with. The Entity Collective is building that space.

Community Program In Development
Find Your People

More Than Competition.

Our vision is a welcoming home for Dead by Daylight players, teams, streamers, coaches, commentators and fans. Whether you play casually or hope to compete, there should be room to participate.

01 / CONNECTION

Meet Other Players

Find people who share your interest in Dead by Daylight, learn from different playstyles and build friendships.

02 / COMPETITION

Community Events

Follow plans for future organized matches, beginner-friendly activities and competitive tournaments.

03 / CREATIVITY

Creators & Streams

Support content creators, discover live streams and collaborate on ideas as the program develops.

04 / DEVELOPMENT

Learn & Improve

Share gameplay insights, discuss Survivor teamwork and Killer strategy, and encourage constructive feedback.

05 / INVOLVEMENT

Help Build TEC

Players and community members may be able to support future events, moderation and organization initiatives.

06 / RECOGNITION

Celebrate Progress

As official events launch, we aim to recognize positive contributions and verified competitive achievements.

Our Standards

Respect in Every Trial.

A strong community starts with clear expectations. Detailed participation and moderation policies will be published before official community channels and events launch.

Respect

Treat other players, organizers and creators with dignity, regardless of experience or competitive result.

Fair Play

Support honest competition, follow published event rules and respect decisions made through a fair review process.

Growth

Make space for newcomers, constructive criticism and opportunities to improve together.
