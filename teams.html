Roster Program In Development
Competitive Divisions

Two Sides. One Collective.

TEC's planned competitive structure recognizes both coordinated Survivor teams and individual Killer specialists. Official rosters and recruitment requirements have not yet been announced.

Division 01 / Team Play

Survivor Teams

Four-player coordination is the foundation of competitive Survivor play. We aim to support teams built on preparation, communication and trust.

  • Teamwork and communication
  • Chase and rescue coordination
  • Objective and resource management
  • Match preparation and strategy
Division 02 / Individual Play

Killer Competitors

Competitive Killers must adapt under pressure, make disciplined decisions and execute consistently against coordinated opponents.

  • Map control and pressure
  • Chase efficiency and game sense
  • Adaptation and decision-making
  • Competitive consistency

The Roster Is Still in the Fog
